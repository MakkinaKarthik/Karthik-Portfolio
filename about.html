About me

Hi, I'm Karthik

I'm a 2026 Computer Science graduate from Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology, Avadi, with a CGPA of 8.3. I'm based in Bengaluru and actively looking for my first full-time role.
